9 个核心功能 9 core features

0101

文字 + 图片 + 位置贴文 Text, images & location posts

280 字以内表达一个完整想法。可以配图增强表达，也可以加上位置标签让贴文更具上下文。 Up to 280 characters for a complete thought. Add images to enrich your message, and an optional location tag for context.

  • 280 字字数统计，所见即所得 Live 280-character counter
  • 最多 4 张图片，支持实时编辑滤镜 Up to 4 images with built-in filters
  • 位置标签可选，不强制 GPS 写入 Optional location, no forced GPS tagging
0202

时间线动态（无算法） Chronological timeline

按时间顺序排列，只展示你选择关注的人。没有"推荐"，没有"热门"，没有信息流操纵。 In chronological order, only from the people you follow. No "For You", no "Trending", no algorithmic manipulation.

  • 严格按时间倒序，不掺杂机器学习推荐 Strict reverse-chronological, no ML ranking
  • 支持"仅关注"和"包含转发"两种视图 Toggle between "Following" and "With Reposts"
  • 可按主题或社区过滤自定义时间线 Filter timeline by topic or community
0303

实时通知 Real-time notifications

点赞、回复、@、关注的实时提醒，不会错过任何重要时刻。 Instant alerts for likes, replies, mentions, and follows — never miss a moment that matters.

  • 推送通知分级：所有人 / 我关注的人 / 关闭 Granular push: everyone, people I follow, off
  • 通知中心按类型聚合：@、点赞、关注、回复 Inbox grouped by type: mentions, likes, follows, replies
  • 静音关键字、话题和用户 Mute keywords, topics, and users
0404

相机集成 Camera integration

原生相机体验，支持前后摄、闪光、超广角、缩放，拍下当下。 Native camera: front, back, flash, ultrawide, zoom — capture the moment as it happens.

  • 前后摄快速切换，单手即可完成 Quick front/back switch, one-handed
  • 支持超广角 0.5x 至 3x 缩放 Ultrawide 0.5x to 3x zoom
  • 闪光灯与实时 HDR 自动模式 Flash and live HDR auto mode
0505

私聊与群聊 DMs & group chat

从 1v1 到最多 50 人的群组消息，支持表情回应和未读提示。 One-to-one and group chats up to 50 people, with reactions and read receipts.

  • 群组消息最多支持 50 人 Group chats up to 50 members
  • 表情回应：👍 ❤️ 🎉 等一键回复 Reactions: 👍 ❤️ 🎉 one-tap replies
  • 端到端加密保护敏感对话 End-to-end encryption for sensitive chats
0606

社区 Communities

加入兴趣社区，与同好互动。每个社区有独立的关注流、规则和版主。 Join interest-based communities, find your people. Each community has its own feed, rules, and moderators.

  • 加入最多 100 个社区，切换视图一目了然 Join up to 100 communities, easy view switching
  • 社区版主可置顶、删除贴文和封禁用户 Mods can pin, remove posts, and ban users
  • 社区规则明确公开，违规有据可依 Clear public rules, enforcement is transparent
0707

话题 Topics

围绕 #话题 聚合内容。点击话题标签即可查看所有相关贴文。 Aggregate content around #topics. Tap a hashtag to see all related posts in one feed.

  • 关注话题以纳入时间线 Follow topics to add them to your timeline
  • "今日热门话题"按真实互动量排序 "Trending" sorted by real engagement
  • 话题详情页：相关社区、推荐关注 Topic detail: related communities, suggested follows
0808

@ 提及与引用 Mentions & quotes

@某人 触发提醒，引用转发让对话公开串联。让一段对话有来有回。 @mention triggers a notification, quote posts weave public conversations together. Threads that go back and forth.

  • @提及触发推送通知 @mention triggers a push notification
  • 引用转发可附加自己的评论 Quote with your own commentary
  • 支持嵌套回复树，对话层层展开 Nested reply threads, layered conversations
0909

多种登录方式 Multiple sign-in options

按你方便的方式开始：Apple ID、Google 账号、手机号，或自选用户名。 Start the way that's convenient: Apple ID, Google, phone number, or pick a username.

  • Sign in with Apple —— 默认隐藏邮箱 Sign in with Apple — hide your email by default
  • Google 一键登录 One-tap Google sign-in
  • 手机号验证码 + 自定义用户名兜底 Phone OTP + custom username fallback
