This Privacy Policy explains how 推文（Tweet轻记） ("we") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your personal information within the mobile application 推文（Tweet轻记） (iOS & Android). 2. 我们收集哪些信息 2. Information We Collect

为了提供和改善服务，我们可能收集以下信息： To provide and improve our services, we may collect the following information:

  • 账号信息：用户名、显示名、头像、邮箱（如使用邮箱注册）或手机号（如使用手机注册）、Apple/Google 登录标识。 Account information: username, display name, avatar, email (if email-registered) or phone (if phone-registered), Apple/Google sign-in identifier.
  • Google 账号信息（Android）：当你选择使用 Google 登录时，我们会收到你的 Google ID Token，用于创建和验证你的账号。 Google account information (Android): when you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your Google ID Token, used to create and verify your account.
  • 出生日期（Android）：注册时必填，用于年龄验证（确保 13 岁以上）。 Date of birth (Android): required at registration for age verification (to ensure users are 13+).
  • 内容信息：你发布的贴文（含文字、图片、可选位置标签）、@提及、回复、点赞、转发等互动数据。 Content: posts you publish (text, images, optional location), @mentions, replies, likes, reposts, and other interaction data.
  • 位置数据：当你主动在帖子中添加位置时，我们会收集你的精确地理位置。 Location data: when you actively add a location to a post, we collect your precise geographic location.
  • 设备与日志：设备型号、系统版本、崩溃日志、IP 地址、访问时间戳（用于安全、调试和性能分析）。 Device & logs: device model, OS version, crash logs, IP address, access timestamps — for security, debugging, and performance analytics.
  • 通讯录（仅 iOS，且需你明确授权后）：用于查找你认识的好友。通讯录数据仅在本机处理，不会上传至我们的服务器。 Contacts (iOS only, with your explicit consent): used to find friends you know. Contact data is processed only on-device and is never uploaded to our servers.

3. 用途 3. How We Use Information

我们使用收集到的信息用于以下目的： We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • 提供、维护、改善推文（Tweet轻记）的核心功能 Providing, maintaining, and improving 推文（Tweet轻记）'s core features
  • 推送通知、消息投递、内容同步 Push notifications, message delivery, content sync
  • 安全防护：识别并阻止滥用、欺诈、违规内容 Security: detecting and preventing abuse, fraud, and policy violations
  • 产品分析：了解功能使用情况、改进体验（数据匿名化或聚合化处理） Analytics: understanding feature usage, improving experience (anonymized or aggregated)
  • 响应你的咨询与反馈 Responding to your inquiries and feedback
  • 我们不会将你的数据出售给第三方广告商，也不会用于跨 App 行为追踪。 We will not sell your data to third-party advertisers, nor use it for cross-app behavioral tracking.

4. 存储和保护 4. Storage and Protection

你的数据存储在受信云服务提供商的安全数据中心，采用静态加密 + 传输加密 双层保护。我们采用业界标准的安全措施，包括： Your data is stored in trusted cloud providers' secure data centers, protected with encryption at rest and in transit. We apply industry-standard security measures including:

  • TLS/HTTPS 全链路加密 TLS / HTTPS end-to-end encryption
  • 数据库静态加密（AES-256） Database-level encryption at rest (AES-256)
  • 最小权限原则的内部访问控制 Least-privilege internal access control
  • 定期安全审计与漏洞修复 Regular security audits and vulnerability patching
  • 账号密码使用强哈希算法（bcrypt）存储 Account passwords stored with strong hashing (bcrypt)

尽管我们尽力保护数据安全，但没有任何系统能保证 100% 安全。如发生数据泄露，我们将在 72 小时内通知你。 While we strive to protect data, no system can be 100% secure. In the event of a data breach, we will notify you within 72 hours.

5. 信息共享 5. Information Sharing

除下列情况外，我们不会与任何第三方共享你的个人信息： Except in the following cases, we will not share your personal information with any third party:

  • 你的公开贴文：默认对所有用户可见（与你选择的可见性设置一致） Your public posts: visible to all users by default (per your visibility setting)
  • 法律要求：当政府部门依法要求披露时 Legal requirement: when government agencies lawfully request disclosure
  • 服务商：仅向为我们提供基础设施的服务商（如云存储、推送服务）披露最少必要信息 Service providers: only the minimum necessary info to infrastructure vendors (e.g. cloud storage, push)
  • 你明确同意的其他情况 Other cases with your explicit consent

6. 用户权利 6. Your Rights

你对自己的数据拥有以下权利： You have the following rights over your data:

  • 访问：随时查看你发布的内容与互动记录 Access: view your content and interaction history anytime
  • 更正：修改你的个人资料 Rectification: correct your profile information
  • 删除：删除已发布的贴文或注销账号（数据将在 30 天内从生产环境清除） Erasure: delete a post or your account (production data purged within 30 days)
  • 导出：以 JSON 格式下载你的数据副本 Portability: download a JSON copy of your data
  • 撤回授权：随时撤回对相机、相册、位置等权限的授权 Revoke: revoke camera, photo, location, etc. permissions anytime

7. 账号注销与数据删除 7. Account Deletion and Data Removal

如果您需要注销账号并删除相关数据，您可以通过以下方式进行： If you need to delete your account and remove associated data, you can do so through the following methods:

  • 应用内注销（推荐）：登录您的账号，前往“我的” -> “设置” -> 找到“注销账号”选项，按照提示操作，您的账号和所有关联数据将在验证后立即删除。 In-App Deletion (Recommended): Log in to your account, go to "Me" -> "Settings" -> find the "Delete Account" option, and follow the prompts. Your account and all associated data will be deleted immediately after verification.
  • 通过电子邮件申请：如果您已经卸载了应用或无法登录，您可以发送申请邮件至我们的官方客服邮箱：support@smspi.com。请在邮件中提供您的注册账号（如手机号或电子邮箱）、申请原因以及联系方式。我们在收到您的注销申请并核实身份后，删除您的个人资料、历史记录及所有相关用户数据。 Apply via Email: If you have already uninstalled the app or cannot log in, you can send an application email to our official customer service email: support@smspi.com. Please provide your registered account (such as phone number or email), the reason for the application, and contact information in the email. Upon receiving your application and verifying your identity, we will delete your personal profile, history, and all associated user data.

10. 权限说明 10. Permissions

推文（Tweet轻记）申请以下系统权限： 推文（Tweet轻记） requests the following system permissions:

  • 相机：用于拍摄并发布图片/视频贴文 Camera: capture and post images/videos
  • 相册：用于选择已拍摄的照片发布 Photo Library: choose existing photos to post
